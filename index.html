About Us

A heritage of trust, traded across borders.

Surani & Sons began as a single cutting bench and a conviction: that a diamond’s value rests as much on the honesty of its seller as on its grade. Today we supply independent jewelers, regional chains and private clients on six continents from cutting centres and design studios across Asia, the Middle East, Europe and North America.

We source rough and polished stones through partners who meet responsible-sourcing standards, and we disclose origin — natural or laboratory-grown — on every invoice. Our workshops finish, set and inspect each piece under magnification before it is sealed, insured and shipped.
