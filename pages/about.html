Our Story

Surani Jewellers & Sons

Surani Jewellers & Sons is a family-rooted jewellery house dedicated exclusively to real jewellery — pure gold, platinum and certified diamonds. We do not deal in imitation, simulants or fashion jewellery.

From traditional bridal sets to contemporary diamond designs and loose polished diamonds, we serve both wholesale partners (retailers, designers, distributors) and individual retail clients across the world.

Our promise is simple: transparent pricing, certified stones, BIS-hallmarked gold, and jewellery that holds its value for generations.

What We Stand For

  • 100% real gold, platinum & diamonds — never imitation
  • Wholesale bulk supply + retail worldwide shipping
  • Certified diamonds (GIA / IGI / HRD)
  • BIS Hallmarked gold with HUID
  • Fair exchange & buyback programmes
  • Lifetime maintenance support
