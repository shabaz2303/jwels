Help & Support

How do I place a wholesale order?

Contact us via the enquiry form selecting “Wholesale / Bulk Order”. Our team will share price lists, MOQs and lead times.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. We supply worldwide for both wholesale and retail clients. Shipping terms and duties are confirmed at the time of order.

Are your diamonds real?

Yes. We only deal in natural and certified lab-grown diamonds. No simulants or imitation stones are sold.

What is the difference between natural and lab-grown?

Natural diamonds are mined from the earth. Lab-grown diamonds (CVD / HPHT) have the same chemical and physical properties but are created in a laboratory. Both are real diamonds.

Can I get a custom design?

Yes. Share your design idea or sketch and we will provide a quotation and timeline.
